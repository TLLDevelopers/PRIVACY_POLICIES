Guess the Country: Globe Game, privacy policy

App: Guess the Country for Android (com.tlldevelopers.guesscountry), by TLL Studios (TLL Developers). Last updated: 18/11/2026. Applies to Guess the Country: Globe Game 1.0.0 and later (the friends league described below is available from version 1.1).

Controller: TLL Studios (TLL Developers). Contact: tll.developers.apps@gmail.com.

Summary

Guess the Country has no accounts unless you join the optional friends league (see below). Your progress, streak, coins and settings stay on your phone. The free version shows ads (Google AdMob), the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase, Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Remote Config for aggregated usage statistics, crash reports and game settings, and everything the game needs (the world map and country names) is bundled in the app.

What Guess the Country stores

Your guesses, daily results, streak, coins, statistics, settings and your Remove ads status are stored only on this device (the country names are matched on the device). We do not receive your answers or your progress. If device backup is on, the system may include them in your personal backup. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Location and permissions

Guess the Country does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or notifications. It uses the internet for ads, usage statistics, crash reports, game settings and purchases.

Ads

The free version shows Google AdMob ads: a banner, occasional full-screen ads between rounds (never in the middle of a round) and, optionally, rewarded videos you can choose to watch. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland we ask for your consent (Google UMP form) before any ad is requested; you can change it in Settings, under Privacy options. Google may use the advertising identifier and approximate location (from the IP address) to serve and measure ads.

Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings > Google > Ads.

The one-time “Remove ads” purchase removes the banners and the between-round ads. Optional rewarded videos (for example to reveal a hint) stay available if you want to watch them.

Usage statistics, crash reports and game settings

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to know, in aggregate, which features are used (for example, how many rounds are started and solved). We use Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash and performance reports (technical trace, device model, system and app version) so that we can fix problems. We use Firebase Remote Config to download game settings (such as prices and feature switches).

Data processed by third-party services

  • Google AdMob: advertising ID / app set ID and device identifiers, approximate location (from the IP address), ad interactions and diagnostics; for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention.
  • Firebase Analytics: app interactions (events and screens viewed), Firebase installation ID / device identifiers, purchase events; for analytics.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash logs and performance diagnostics; for analytics and app stability.
  • Firebase Remote Config: Firebase installation ID and app version, to deliver game settings.
  • Google Play Billing: handles the purchase; we only learn whether it is valid.

Data sent to these services is encrypted in transit (TLS). This matches the Google Play Data safety section of the app.

Friends league (optional, from version 1.1)

The friends league only exists if you tap “Join the league”. Joining creates an anonymous Firebase Authentication account (a random user ID, no email, no phone number, no name) and stores on Firebase Cloud Firestore: a nickname and avatar generated by the app (you cannot type free text), your friend code, your friendships and, for each daily you finish, its result (solved or not, number of guesses, hints used, your streak and the time it was recorded). We do not store your name, email, location, contacts or the country you were looking for.

Only friends you have accepted can read your results, and only your nickname, avatar and results are visible to them. You can remove or block anyone at any time. The app deletes your own results after about two weeks, and we also purge expired data from the server periodically.

You can delete all your league data (nickname, friendships, results, blocks and the anonymous account) in Settings > Delete my league data. You can also ask us to do it by writing to tll.developers.apps@gmail.com and quoting your friend code. The optional reminder “friends have played” is a notification created on your phone; no notification is sent from a server. If you open an invitation link, the Google Play Install Referrer API gives the app the friend code that was in the link, once, after installing.

Purchases

The Remove ads purchase is processed by Google Play and charged to your Google account. Guess the Country does not receive your payment details; it only learns whether the purchase is valid, so that it can be restored on a new device.

Children

Guess the Country is a general-audience geography game and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Guess the Country does not ask for any account information, and the friends league uses only generated nicknames.

Your rights and data deletion

If you never joined the friends league there is nothing on our servers to delete. If you joined, delete your league data in Settings > Delete my league data, or write to tll.developers.apps@gmail.com quoting your friend code. Uninstall the app to remove everything stored on your phone. For data processed by Google (ads, analytics, crash reports) you can use Google’s privacy controls, and for any request you can write to tll.developers.apps@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
