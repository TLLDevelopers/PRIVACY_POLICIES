Mini Sudoku: privacy policy

App: Mini Sudoku for Android (com.tlldevelopers.minisudoku), by TLL Studios (TLL Developers). Last updated: 19/11/2026. Applies to Mini Sudoku 1.0.0 and later (the friends league from version 1.2.0).

Controller: TLL Studios (TLL Developers). Contact: tll.developers.apps@gmail.com.

Summary

Mini Sudoku has no accounts (unless you join the optional friends league, see below) and no server of its own. Your puzzle progress, statistics, settings and daily streak stay on your phone. The free version shows ads (Google AdMob) and the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics for aggregated usage statistics and crash reports.

What Mini Sudoku stores

Progress, statistics, achievements, settings and your Remove ads status are stored only on this device. If device backup is on, the system may include them in your personal backup. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Friends league (optional, from version 1.2)

The friends league only exists if you tap “Join the league”. Joining creates an anonymous Firebase Authentication account (a random user ID, no email, no phone number, no name) and stores on Google Cloud Firestore: a nickname and avatar generated by the app (you cannot type free text), your friend code, your friendships and, for each daily puzzle you solve, its result (your solve time, the number of mistakes and hints used, your streak and the time it was recorded). We do not store your name, email, location, contacts or your board.

Only friends you have accepted can read your results, and only your nickname, avatar and results are visible to them. You can remove or block anyone at any time. The app deletes your own results after about two weeks, and we also purge expired data from the server periodically.

You can delete all your league data (nickname, friendships, results, blocks and the anonymous account) in Settings > Delete my league data. You can also ask us to do it by writing to tll.developers.apps@gmail.com and quoting your friend code. The optional reminder “friends have played” is a notification created on your phone; no notification is sent from a server. If you open an invitation link, the Google Play Install Referrer API gives the app the friend code that was in the link, once, after installing.

Location and permissions

Mini Sudoku does not ask for your location, contacts, camera or microphone. It can ask for permission to show notifications (Android 13 and later) for an optional daily puzzle reminder; the reminder is scheduled on your device and no server is involved, and you can turn it off in the app or in Android settings. It uses the internet for ads, usage statistics, crash reports and purchases.

Ads

The free version shows Google AdMob ads: a banner, occasional full-screen ads between puzzles (never in the middle of a puzzle) and, optionally, rewarded videos you can choose to watch. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland we ask for your consent (Google UMP form) before showing ads; you can change it in Settings when the option is shown. Google may use the advertising identifier and approximate location (from the IP address) to serve and measure ads.

Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings > Google > Ads.

The one-time “Remove ads” purchase removes the banners and the between-puzzle ads. Optional rewarded videos stay available if you want to watch them.

Usage statistics and crash reports

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to know, in aggregate, which features are used (for example, how many puzzles are started and completed). We use Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash and performance reports (technical trace, device model, system and app version) so that we can fix problems.

Data processed by third-party services

  • Google AdMob: advertising ID / app set ID and device identifiers, approximate location (from the IP address), ad interactions and diagnostics; for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention.
  • Firebase Analytics: app interactions (events and screens viewed), Firebase installation ID / device identifiers, purchase events; for analytics.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash logs and performance diagnostics; for analytics and app stability.
  • Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore (only if you join the friends league): an anonymous user ID and the league data described above; for app functionality. Not shared with third parties and not used for advertising.

Data sent to these services is encrypted in transit (TLS). This matches the Google Play Data safety section of the app.

Purchases

The Remove ads purchase is processed by Google Play and charged to your Google account. Mini Sudoku does not receive your payment details; it only learns whether the purchase is valid.

Children

Mini Sudoku is a general-audience puzzle and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Mini Sudoku does not ask for any account information.

Your rights and data deletion

The app has no accounts, so there is nothing on our servers to delete unless you joined the friends league: in that case, delete your league data in Settings > Delete my league data, or write to us quoting your friend code. Uninstall the app to remove everything stored on your phone. For data processed by Google (ads, analytics, crash reports) you can use Google’s privacy controls, and for any request you can write to tll.developers.apps@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
