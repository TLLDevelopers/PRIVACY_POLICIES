Tidbo: privacy policy

App: Tidbo: Sort Puzzle Game for Android (com.tlldevelopers.shelflogic), by TLL Studios (TLL Developers). Last updated: 25/11/2026. Applies to Tidbo 0.4.0 and later.

Controller: TLL Studios (TLL Developers). Contact: tll.developers.apps@gmail.com.

Summary

Tidbo has no accounts and no server of its own. Your progress, coins and settings stay on your phone. The game shows ads (Google AdMob), and the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Remote Config for aggregated usage statistics and to choose between game settings.

What Tidbo stores

Your level progress, stars, coins, daily streak, sound and notification settings are stored only on this device. If device backup is on, the system may include them in your personal backup. Uninstalling the app deletes them. Tidbo never asks for your name, email, location, contacts, camera or microphone.

Notifications

Tidbo can show one optional daily reminder (for example when your streak is waiting or a chest is ready). It is a local notification generated on your phone; no push server is involved. The app asks for permission once, and you can turn the reminder off in Settings or in Android settings.

Sharing a result

When you tap share, the app creates a text with your result and opens the system share sheet. You choose where it goes. Tidbo does not upload it.

Ads

Tidbo is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: a full-screen ad between some levels and optional videos that you can watch to earn extra coins or a hint. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland we ask for your consent (Google UMP form) before showing ads; you can change it later where the option is shown. Google may use the advertising identifier and approximate location (from the IP address) to serve and measure ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings > Google > Ads.

Usage statistics and game settings

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to know, in aggregate, how the game is used (for example, which levels are started or completed, how long they take, whether a hint is used and how many levels players reach). We use Firebase Remote Config to switch game settings, such as which visual theme a new player gets. These services use a Firebase installation ID and device identifiers.

Data processed by third-party services

  • Google AdMob: advertising ID / app set ID and device identifiers, approximate location (from the IP address), ad interactions and diagnostics; for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention.
  • Firebase Analytics and Remote Config: app interactions, Firebase installation ID / device identifiers, diagnostics; for analytics and app functionality.

Data sent to these services is encrypted in transit (TLS). This matches the Google Play Data safety section of the app.

Permissions

  • Internet: for ads, usage statistics and game settings.
  • Notifications (optional): for the daily reminder.
  • Vibration: light haptic feedback while playing.
  • Run at startup: to restore the daily reminder after the phone restarts.

Children

Tidbo is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Tidbo does not ask for any account information.

Your rights and data deletion

The app has no accounts, so there is nothing on our servers to delete. Uninstall the app to remove everything stored on your phone. For data processed by Google (ads and analytics) you can use Google’s privacy controls, and for any request you can write to tll.developers.apps@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
